# The EyeKeeper gallery

[← Documentation](README.md) · [Settings](USER-GUIDE.md) · [Download](https://github.com/Ali-zeiynali/EyeKeeper/releases/latest)

18 atmospheres. 8 flowing materials. **144 palette/material combinations.** Choose either independently, or randomize both for a different mood at your next break. Click any still to open the full-size render.

## In motion

![Animated liquid reminder and rest sequence](previews/06-liquid-demo.webp)

These are offscreen renders of the same WPF controls and a CPU reference of the shader equations. The two-display layout is simulated; previews are illustrative and are not a hardware benchmark.
